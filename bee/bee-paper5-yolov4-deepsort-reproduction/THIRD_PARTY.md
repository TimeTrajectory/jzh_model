# Third-party components

- AlexeyAB Darknet is pinned to commit `59596d7880f6504768df41d6daa586f5cb2b932f`. Its own license and notices remain in `vendor/darknet/` after setup.
- `yolov4.conv.137` is the official YOLOv4 convolutional initialization distributed with Darknet.
- BEE22 belongs to the authors of *Intelligent beehive monitoring system based on internet of things and colony state analysis*. This project references the local dataset prepared by paper 2 and does not commit its images.
- `deep-sort-realtime` implements DeepSORT-compatible online tracking and retains its upstream license.
- The source paper PDF stays in `/jzk-model/pdf-paper/31356.pdf` and is not redistributed by this project.

The MIT license at the project root covers only the new reproduction glue code and documentation.
